Root Cause Chain

1
Static Paper Timetables

Dispatchers ("นายท่า") release buses on fixed clock intervals without real-time data

2
Crowding Dwell-Time Feedback Loop

Delayed lead bus fills to >85% capacity → boarding slows by 1.35× → dwells longer at every stop

3
Trailing Bus Catches Up

Next bus on fixed clock encounters cleared stops → catches lead bus → 2–3 buses bumper-to-bumper

4
25–35 Minute Gap Behind

Passengers at downstream stops face long waits, then crush-loaded buses

Why not mid-route holding? Our 840-run Digital Twin benchmark proved mid-route holding worsened Total Passenger Time by +652.83 person-seconds. The depot is the only intervention point with 0% onboard passenger harm.

Slide 3 of 18 · Problem Statement (cont.)

Why the Depot Dispatcher ("นายท่า") Is the Single Intervention Point

❌ Passenger Apps — Report the Problem After It Happens

Commuters see 3 buses stuck together 4 km away followed by a 30-minute gap. Knowing the bus is bunched does not make it come faster. Fixing spacing requires acting at the supply source — before the bus enters the corridor.

❌ Traffic Signals — Outside Operator Control

Major Bangkok intersections (Phaya Thai, Pratunam, Ratchaprasong) are manually held for 3–5+ minutes by traffic police. A bus operator cannot control police signal boxes.

✅ Depot Departure — 100% Operator Control, 0% Passenger Harm

840-run SHA-256 hash-verified benchmark: depot departure adjustment saved -660.00 person-seconds across 30/30 disrupted seeds (100% consistency) with 0% mid-route passenger harm.

Sources: [participant], [TDRI & OTP public transit studies]
Slide 4 of 18 · Solution Summary

Evenflow: Three-Layer Innovation

100% B2B/B2G Depot Dispatch Decision-Support — Zero Consumer App, Zero Street Hardware

Layer 1 Real-Time 90° Overhead Edge-AI Crowding Measurement

YOLOv8-Nano + ByteTrack non-facial silhouette counting above bus doors. +In/-Out counts in volatile RAM. 100% PDPA-compliant, zero video storage. Bayesian Door-Dwell Cross-Check: 0.85 × CameraCount + 0.15 × ImpliedDwell.

Layer 2 8.6-Minute Early-Warning Predictor

Forecasts downstream gaps (>22 min), bunching (<5 min), and overcrowding (>85% load) 516.17 seconds (~8.6 min) ahead with 1.00 precision (zero false alarms) and 78.21s headway MAE. Applies 1.35× crowding dwell-inflation when cabin load exceeds 75%.

Layer 3 Counterfactual Digital Twin Depot Optimizer

Parallel Node.js Worker Threads (2.47 ms latency) simulate departure adjustments (-3 to +5 min) across 75%/100%/125% demand scenarios. Dispatcher approves with 1-Click. Hard guardrails: Bus in Bay, EV SoC ≥ 35%, Driver Rest ≥ 6 min, No-Blame ค่าเที่ยว protection.

Sources: [participant], [Drabicki et al. 2023], [Daganzo 2009]

Slide 16 of 18 · Key Risks & Mitigations

Key Risks & Mitigations

R1 — All Inbound Buses Delayed by Severe Gridlock

Med / High

No physical bus in depot bay for early release.

Mitigation: Inbound Turnaround Tracker + Proactive Pre-Gap Pacing: tracks returning buses 20–30 min ahead; spaces out remaining depot buses (+2 to +5 min); alerts dispatcher for reserve bus injection.

R2 — Dispatcher / Driver Resistance

Med / High

Fear that AI adjustments will cut rest or reduce ค่าเที่ยว income.

Mitigation: 100% Human-in-the-Loop + Hard Labor Locks: ≥6 min rest lock, No-Blame Charter (ไม่หักเงินอัตโนมัติ · ไม่เสียค่าเที่ยว), 1-second ribbon, Emergency Kill Switch.

R3 — Camera Occlusion or Vehicle Power Surge

Low-Med / Med

Heavy rain/umbrellas at doorway or 24V crank surges.

Mitigation: Bayesian Vision-Dwell Cross-Check (0.85×Cam + 0.15×ImpliedDwell) + zero-reset at turnaround + 9–36V DC automotive surge regulator & <10W 45°C heatsink.

R4 — State Enterprise Procurement Delays (ขสมก.)

Med / Med

Long public tender cycles for BMTA.

Mitigation: Bottom-Up Campus/Private Depot Beachhead: launch at single-decision-maker University Campus Shuttle (มธ. รังสิต via TU-RAC) or Private EV Depot (2–3 week sign-off, <฿210k) to generate audited ROI before BMTA expansion.

R5 — Doorway Crowd Occlusion & Counting Drift

Peak rush-hour drops raw accuracy ~97% → ~88–90%.

Fix: Bayesian cross-check + zero-reset at turnaround + Weeks 1–3 Shadow Calibration vs. manual spotters until ≥95%.

R6 — Harsh Bus Electrical & Thermal Environment

45°C+ cabin, vibration, 24V crank surges.

Fix: ~฿22k kit: 9–36V buck-boost + 10s supercapacitor + M12 vibration brackets + fanless <10W heatsink + SQLite Store-and-Forward.
